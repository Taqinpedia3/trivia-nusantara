**Description (437/500 characters)**

I built Trivia Nusantara, a real-time quiz for 1–8 players, with solo play and room-code multiplayer. Players race through 10 timed questions about Indonesian food, travel, and culture. Faster correct answers earn more points, and everyone sees a shared leaderboard after each round. I learned how to keep answers private while syncing timers and scores. Next, I plan to add more regional question packs and polish the mobile experience.
